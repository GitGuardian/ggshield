### Added

- `honeytoken plant` can now plant kubeconfig honeytokens (`--type kubeconfig`,
  `--method kubeconfig`). The decoy context, cluster and user are merged into
  `~/.kube/config` next to the user's real entries and removed again when the
  honeytoken is revoked; `current-context` is never changed, so the owner's own
  `kubectl` cannot trip the decoy.
