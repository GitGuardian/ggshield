### Added

- ggshield can now read, write and inject secrets from your secret manager:
  `ggshield secret get`, `set`, `del`, `import`, `encrypt`, `run`, `activate`
  and `trust`. HashiCorp Vault, 1Password Connect and dotenv files encrypted
  with a device-local key are supported, and `ggshield secret run -- <command>`
  passes the secrets to a command as environment variables without ever
  writing them to disk.
